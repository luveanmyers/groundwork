LUV-42: sharing a trip. A new Share button sits under the trip's name and dates on every tab of a trip (frame 1, dashed outline). It opens a panel that slides up from the bottom, listing who's on the trip, with a box to add someone by email (frames 2-4). Anyone added gets full edit access to the whole trip (ideas, itinerary, packing, budget and notes) as soon as they sign in with Google using that email. Once someone else is on the trip, the button reads "Shared with 1" (or 2, 3...).
Not in this version: leaving a trip yourself (you can't remove your own row), and seeing whether the other person has signed in yet. The app can't look that up without a server of its own.
