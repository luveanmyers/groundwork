MOCKUP - not the live app. Sample Scotland data only (trip dates Jul 29 - Aug 10, 2027), nothing here is saved.
LUV-24: dates outside the trip. The only date pickers on cards are the Flight/Transport departure and arrival dates and the Lodging check-in and check-out dates. Plain activities have no date field: they get a day by being dragged onto the calendar, which already only accepts days inside the trip.
Recommended: warn, don't block. Any of those dates outside the trip's range gets an amber note right under it, and the date still saves. The note says whether the card will still show on the calendar, and has a Change trip dates link that opens the Edit trip sheet (LUV-50). The same note shows everywhere the fields appear: the add form (1), the card on the Ideas tab (2) and the calendar popover (3).
Why not block? The case it would break is Scotland's own first card: an overnight flight that leaves the day before the trip's first day. You may also want to record a booking before the trip's dates are final. Blocking would mean changing the trip's dates first, every time. The alternative is described at the bottom (4).

4 · Alternative (not recommended): block the dates

The date pickers would grey out every day outside Jul 29 - Aug 10, so they can't be picked at all, and no note is needed. The overnight flight in (1) couldn't be entered with its real departure date until the trip's start date moved to Jul 28.
