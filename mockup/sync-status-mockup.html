  • Signing out with changes still waiting to sync would lose them, so it asks first (frame 7).
  • Unchanged: the ☰ menu keeps "Signed in as … / Sign out" at the bottom. Its Sign out gets the same frame-7 check.
The four states (exact wording)
SyncedOnline, and every change made on this device has reached the server. This is what you'll see nearly all the time.
Syncing…Online and a change is on its way. Only shown if it takes more than 2 seconds, so ordinary edits don't make the label flicker.
Waiting to syncChanges are saved on this device but haven't reached the server: you're offline, or a change has been stuck for 10+ seconds (phones sometimes claim to be online on weak wifi). They send themselves when there's signal. The only amber state: it means "Dave can't see this yet".
OfflineNo signal, nothing waiting. Everything still works; your edits and Dave's swap when you're back online.
