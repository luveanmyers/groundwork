What changed from v1
  • The bottom row now shows where you sleep each night. A stay's bar ends on its last night, not on check-out day. So "Edinburgh flat, check in Jul 6, check out Jul 9" covers Jul 6, 7 and 8. The bar shows "In" time on its first night and "Out" time on its last.
  • A night with nothing booked gets a red No lodging flag in that same row. The separate "Gaps" row is gone.
  • Transport needed is a small red chip at the start of the stay you're travelling to. Click it to add a note, mark it sorted, or add a Transport card.
  • Try the row name below. "Overnight" is the starting suggestion.
Example trip: Scotland, Tue Jul 6 – Sat Jul 17, 2027.
  • Night of Jul 9: nothing booked → No lodging. Edinburgh → Highlands is covered by the dated "Train to Inverness" card, so no transport chip.
  • Night of Jul 13: nothing booked → No lodging. Highlands → Skye has no dated Flight or Transport card, so the Portree bar gets a red Transport chip. The two-day Highland tour above doesn't count as lodging.
  • Skye → Glasgow (Jul 16): already marked sorted with a note, so its chip is grey.
  • Jul 17 is the last day: no night, so no flag.
