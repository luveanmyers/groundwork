v2: lining Location up with Lodging (your test finding). In v1, Edinburgh covered Jul 6–8 as whole days, but the Edinburgh flat runs from check-in on the evening of Jul 6 to check-out on the morning of Jul 9 (the half-day Lodging row from LUV-29). So the two were half a day apart. Use the Location bars switch below to compare:
  • Half-days (recommended): on a day the place changes, the morning belongs to where you woke up and the evening to where you sleep. Edinburgh now runs to the morning of Jul 9, the morning you check out, and Stirling starts that evening. The flat sits under Edinburgh exactly, apart from Jul 6's morning: that's arrival day, before check-in. What's saved doesn't change: still one place per day, meaning where you sleep that night.
  • Whole days (v1): each place fills whole days. Simpler, but a stay always looks half a day off from its location.
What's new: a Location row right under the dates. It's where you plan which region you'll be in each day, before anything is booked. Try it:
  • Click an empty Location cell (Sat Jul 17) and type. Suggestions come from regions already on your cards and locations already used on other days. Typing "glasgow" saves as "Glasgow", matching the card spelling, so the same place doesn't end up spelled two ways.
  • Days in a row with the same location join into one bar, like All Day bars do. Use + and − on a bar to stretch it to the next day or pull it back. × clears it.
  • Click a bar to rename it or clear it. A rename changes every day in that bar.
  • Gap flags: the night of Tue Jul 13 now reads "Pick one: 2 options in The Plan". The two Fort William hotels in The Plan have no dates yet, but their region matches the plan for that night. Before this, only dated options counted, so it said "No lodging". The other two flag ideas from the ticket are behind the switches below so you can see them; I recommend leaving both off for now.
