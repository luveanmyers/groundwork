LUV-50: edit a trip after it's created. A new Edit button sits beside Share under the trip's name, on every tab (frame 1, dashed outline). It opens the same slide-up sheet as Share, holding the New Trip form's four fields, filled in: name, destination, start and end date (frame 2).
Shrinking the dates never deletes or moves anything. A card placed on a day that's no longer in the trip keeps its day and slot. It waits in a new Outside the trip dates group in Itinerary Building Blocks (frame 5) until you drag it back onto the calendar or widen the dates again. So instead of an "are you sure?" pop-up, the sheet shows a note naming what will end up outside (frame 3).
Today's bug this also fixes: a card dated outside the trip currently disappears from both the calendar and the sidebar. And a trip created without dates gets an Add dates button on its Itinerary tab (frame 6), so it's no longer stuck.
